What are Font Tools?

This collection helps you work with typefaces. Font Identifier currently supports finding similar fonts from an image. For decorative Unicode text, browse Font Styles instead.

How to get started

  1. Open Font Identifier and choose a clear text image.
  2. Select one line of text for matching.
  3. Compare candidates and open a font's details.

A few useful answers.

Are Font Tools free to use?

The current Font Identifier is free and processes images locally in your browser.

Will it identify an exact commercial font?

It compares against a supported Google Fonts collection. Commercial fonts and custom lettering may have no exact match.
