What happens next?

Font Identifier compares the selected text with a supported Google Fonts collection. You can then compare the closest candidates beside your original image. A similar result is a starting point, not a guaranteed identification.

What if my image contains several fonts?

Choose a line containing just one typeface. Repeat with another line to compare a different font.
