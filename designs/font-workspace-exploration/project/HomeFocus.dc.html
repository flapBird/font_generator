How to use it

  1. Type your words and choose a category.
  2. Tap a sample to copy, or open its preview.
  3. Paste into your app and check the result.
Why do some characters show as boxes?

Your destination app may not have a font that supports those characters. Try a simpler style and check the result on the device where it will be read.

Are these font files?

This tool copies text. For PNG artwork, choose Visual & Art. AI Font is a separate font-file service with sign-in and credit requirements.
